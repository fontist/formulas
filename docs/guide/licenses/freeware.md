# Freeware (Personal Use Only)

[![License: Freeware](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-Freeware-007bff)](#) [![Category: Freely Distributable](https://img.shields.io/badge/Category-Freely%20Distributable-007bff)]

**SPDX Identifier:** Not on SPDX

## Quick Summary

"Freeware" fonts are free to use personally but typically have restrictions on redistribution, modification, or commercial use. Terms vary by font - always check the specific license.

## Permissions Overview

### ✅ Typically Allows

| Context | Permission | Notes |
|---------|------------|-------|
| Academic | ⚠️ Varies | Check specific terms |
| Non-commercial | ✅ Usually | Personal use typically allowed |
| Desktop (Personal) | ✅ Usually | |

### ⚠️ Conditional - Varies by Font

| Permission | Typical Condition |
|------------|-------------------|
| Commercial use | May require paid license |
| Redistribution | Often prohibited |
| Modification | Usually prohibited |
| Web embedding | May require separate license |

### ❌ Typically Disallows

| Permission | Notes |
|------------|-------|
| Redistribution | Often explicitly prohibited |
| Modification | Usually not allowed |
| Sale | Cannot sell font files |
| Commercial use | May require paid license |

## Usage Guidance

For freeware fonts:

1. **Read the license carefully** - Terms vary significantly
2. **Check the font file** - License info may be embedded
3. **Visit the foundry website** - For current terms
4. **Consider purchasing** - For commercial use rights
5. **Document everything** - Keep license records

## Common Freeware Scenarios

### Personal Blog
- Often allowed, but check if attribution is required

### Commercial Website
- May require purchasing a commercial license

### Print Publication
- May require extended license

### App/Game Development
- Usually requires separate embedding license

## Important Warning

"Freeware" does not mean "public domain" or "unrestricted":
- Copyright still applies
- License terms must be followed
- Violations can have legal consequences
