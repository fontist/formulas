# Public Domain

[![License: Public Domain](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-Public%20Domain-28a745)](#) [![Category: Open Source](https://img.shields.io/badge/Category-Open%20Source-28a745)]

**SPDX Identifier:** Not on SPDX (not a license)

## Quick Summary

Fonts explicitly dedicated to the public domain have no copyright restrictions whatsoever. The creator has relinquished all rights, allowing unrestricted use, modification, and redistribution.

## Difference from CC0

| Aspect | Public Domain | CC0 |
|--------|--------------|-----|
| Legal mechanism | Direct dedication | License-based dedication |
| Jurisdictions | May not work everywhere | Designed to work globally |
| Revocability | Generally irrevocable | Explicitly irrevocable |
| Legal certainty | Varies by jurisdiction | More certain legal backing |

## Usage Examples

### What You CAN Do

- Anything at all - no restrictions apply
- Use without attribution
- Modify and claim as your own
- Sell the fonts
- Remove all copyright notices
- Use in any project, any purpose
- Include in proprietary products

### What You CANNOT Do

Nothing - this is public domain with no restrictions.

## Important Considerations

### Jurisdictional Issues

Some jurisdictions do not recognize:
- The ability to place works in the public domain during the author's lifetime
- The ability to waive moral rights
- The concept of public domain at all

In such jurisdictions, the work may still have some restrictions.

### Proving Public Domain Status

When a font is marked as public domain:
- Document where you obtained it
- Save the author's dedication statement
- Keep records for legal protection

### Mislabeling Warning

Not all fonts marked "public domain" actually are:
- Some use the term loosely
- Some have hidden restrictions
- Some are mislabeled by third parties

Always verify with the original source.
