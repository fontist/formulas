**SPDX Identifier:** `IPA`
**SPDX Page:** https://spdx.org/licenses/IPA.html

## Quick Summary

The IPA Font License is used for Japanese fonts developed by the Information-technology Promotion Agency (IPA) of Japan. It allows free use and redistribution with some conditions.
