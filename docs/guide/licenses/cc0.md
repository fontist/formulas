## Quick Summary

CC0 (Creative Commons Zero) is a public domain dedication. Works under CC0 have no copyright restrictions whatsoever - you can do anything with them without attribution or conditions.

## Usage Examples

### What You CAN Do

- Anything - no restrictions apply
- Use without attribution
- Modify and claim as your own
- Sell the fonts
- Remove all copyright notices
- Use in any project, any purpose

### What You CANNOT Do

Nothing - this is public domain.

## Important Notes

- CC0 is irrevocable - once applied, cannot be withdrawn
- Some jurisdictions don't recognize public domain dedications
- In those cases, CC0 acts as a permissive license
